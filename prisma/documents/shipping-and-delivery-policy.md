# Harbor & Pine — Shipping & Delivery Policy

_Last revised: January 2026. Applies to all orders placed on harborandpine.com, Amazon, and retail partners._

## 1. Processing times

Orders placed before 1:00 PM Central Time on a business day are processed the same day. Orders placed after that cutoff, or on a weekend or observed holiday, are processed the next business day.

Made-to-order items — currently the Solid Oak Storage Bench (HP-STG-018) and the Handwoven Jute Rug 5x8 (HP-RUG-007) — require an additional 3 to 5 business days in production before they ship. This is stated on the product page and in the order confirmation email.

## 2. Delivery estimates by service

| Service | Carrier | Estimate after dispatch |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Ground | UPS, USPS | 4 to 7 business days |
| Expedited | UPS, FedEx | 2 to 3 business days |
| Freight (over 50 lb) | FedEx Freight | 7 to 12 business days |

Delivery estimates are business days and exclude the day of dispatch. Estimates are not guarantees except where an order was placed with a Guaranteed Delivery Date at checkout.

## 3. When an order is late

An order is considered **delayed** when it has passed its estimated delivery date and the carrier has not marked it delivered.

- **1 to 3 days past estimate.** Carrier networks routinely recover within this window. Support may reassure the customer but should not issue compensation.
- **4 to 7 days past estimate.** The customer is entitled to a refund of any shipping charge paid on the order, on request. Support may issue this without escalation.
- **More than 7 days past estimate, or the carrier reports the parcel as lost.** The order is treated as lost in transit. See section 5.

## 4. Delivery exceptions

A delivery exception means the carrier attempted delivery and could not complete it, or the parcel was damaged, misrouted, or refused.

When a carrier reports **damaged in transit** and the parcel is being returned to sender, the customer must not be asked to wait for the return to complete. Harbor & Pine treats the order as failed at the moment the exception is posted and resolves it under section 5 immediately.

Address-related exceptions — inaccessible address, no safe location, business closed — are resolved by contacting the customer to confirm the address and requesting redelivery. These do not entitle the customer to compensation unless the address on file was correct and the carrier erred.

## 5. Lost, damaged in transit, and failed deliveries

Where an order is lost, damaged in transit, or otherwise failed through no fault of the customer, the customer chooses one of:

1. **A replacement**, dispatched at expedited service at no charge, or
2. **A full refund** of the order, including any shipping charge paid.

Support does not require the customer to file a claim with the carrier. Harbor & Pine files the carrier claim internally.

A full refund under this section may be issued without manager approval up to **$600.00**. Above that amount, a manager approves first.

## 6. Delivered but not received

Where a carrier has marked a parcel delivered and the customer reports not receiving it, support waits 48 hours from the delivery scan before acting, because late-scan deliveries frequently appear within that window. After 48 hours the order is treated as lost under section 5.
