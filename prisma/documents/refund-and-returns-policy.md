# Harbor & Pine — Refund & Returns Policy

_Last revised: February 2026._

## 1. The standard return window

Customers may return most items within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund of the item price. The 30 days run from the carrier's delivery scan, not from the order date.

Returns are free where the return is caused by Harbor & Pine — a damaged, defective, or incorrect item. Where the return is a change of mind, a flat **$9.95** return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.

## 2. Items that cannot be returned

- Items marked Final Sale at the time of purchase.
- Custom or personalised items, including monogrammed linens.
- Candles and other consumables where the seal has been broken.

These exclusions do not apply where the item arrived damaged or defective. A damaged Final Sale item is still refunded in full.

## 3. Refund amounts

A refund never exceeds what was actually paid on the order, less any amount already refunded against it.

- **Full refund.** The order total including shipping. Issued where the order was lost, arrived damaged, or was incorrect.
- **Partial refund.** Issued where the customer keeps a damaged item at a discount, or where only part of a multi-item order is affected. The amount is the affected item's line total.
- **Shipping-only refund.** Issued where the goods arrived intact but late, per section 3 of the Shipping & Delivery Policy.

## 4. Approval thresholds

| Amount | Who approves |
|---|---|
| Up to $600.00 | Any support agent |
| $600.01 to $2,000.00 | Support manager |
| Over $2,000.00 | Head of Operations |

Every refund is recorded with the order number, the amount, the reason, and the person who approved it. A refund issued without a recorded reason is treated as an audit failure.

## 5. Refund timing

Refunds are submitted to the original payment method on the day they are approved. Card issuers typically post the credit within 5 to 10 business days. Support should quote this range and should not promise a specific date.

## 6. Goodwill credits

Where a customer is unhappy but no refund is owed under this policy, support may offer a goodwill store credit up to **$50.00** without approval. Goodwill credits are not refunds and are not issued to the payment method.
