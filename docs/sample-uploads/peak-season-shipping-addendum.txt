HARBOR & PINE — PEAK SEASON SHIPPING ADDENDUM 2026
Internal operations bulletin. Supersedes the standard Shipping & Delivery Policy
where the two conflict, for the period 20 November 2026 to 10 January 2027 only.

1. ORDER CUTOFFS FOR GUARANTEED DECEMBER 24 DELIVERY

  Standard Ground .......... order by December 15, 1:00 PM CT
  Expedited ................ order by December 19, 1:00 PM CT
  Freight (over 50 lb) ..... order by December 8, 1:00 PM CT
  Made-to-order items ...... order by December 5, 1:00 PM CT

Orders placed after these cutoffs are not covered by the guarantee and support
must not promise a December 24 arrival on them, regardless of what the checkout
estimate displayed.

2. RELAXED DELAY THRESHOLDS

During peak season, carrier networks routinely run 2 to 3 days behind. The delay
tiers in the standard policy are widened accordingly:

  1 to 5 days past estimate ..... no compensation, reassure the customer
  6 to 10 days past estimate .... shipping charge refunded on request
  More than 10 days ............. treat as lost, full refund under section 5

These widened tiers do NOT apply to orders that carry a Guaranteed Delivery Date,
and they do NOT apply where the carrier has posted a damage or loss exception.
A damage exception is resolved immediately at any time of year.

3. ELEVATED APPROVAL CEILING

The support agent full-refund ceiling is temporarily raised from $600.00 to
$900.00 for the duration of this addendum, to reduce escalation load. The manager
tier ($900.01 to $2,000.00) and Head of Operations tier (over $2,000.00) are
unchanged.

4. CARRIER CAPACITY NOTES

UPS has confirmed capacity through December 20. FedEx Freight has advised that
residential freight deliveries will pause between December 22 and December 27.
USPS has no stated cutoff but historically degrades badly after December 18; do
not route anything time-sensitive to USPS after that date.

5. RETURNS WINDOW EXTENSION

Any order delivered between November 20 and December 24 carries an extended
return window through January 31, 2027, rather than the standard 30 days. The
$9.95 change-of-mind return fee still applies.
